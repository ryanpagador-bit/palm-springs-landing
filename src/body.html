The layout

Four Rancho villa neighbourhoods sit at the centre, on either side of Canyon Park and its waterways. Eleven Aliso townhouse neighbourhoods wrap around them. Green corridors, the Central Spine and Sunset Avenue link it all on foot.

Phase 1: where our picks are

Next steps · Nakheel process

How to register your interest (EOI).

  1. 01Sign the form

    We email you the EOI form. Fill it in, sign it and send it back with a copy of your passport, plus Emirates ID if you live in the UAE.

  2. 02Get your reference

    We submit your EOI to Nakheel. You receive an Application Reference Number by email: keep it, as every payment must quote it.

  3. 03Pay the deposit

    By bank transfer, uploading proof through the link in your reference email. Or by card or cheque at the Meraas-Nakheel Sales Centre in Al Sufouh or the City Walk boutique.

  4. 04You are registered

    Your EOI shows as Submitted until the deposit clears, then Paid. That confirms your interest ahead of launch.

Paying by cheque: manager's cheques are held and not cashed; personal cheques are banked and cashed.

What does it take to book?

10% of the price plus the 4% DLD fee: AED 532k on the 3-bed townhouse, AED 728k on the 4-bed G+2 and AED 1.25M on the 5-bed villa, on starting prices.

Is it on Palm Jebel Ali?

No. It's on Sheikh Zayed Road at the gateway to Palm Jebel Ali, about 14 minutes' drive away.

Can I mortgage the handover payment?

Yes. The 40% due on handover can be mortgaged, and nothing is due between May 2029 and handover.

Does signing up commit me to buy?

No. It's free and doesn't commit you to anything.
